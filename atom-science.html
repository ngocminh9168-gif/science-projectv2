Key Concepts Summary

Atomic Structure

Nucleus (protons + neutrons) at center. Electrons in shells around nucleus. Protons = positive charge, electrons = negative charge, neutrons = neutral.

Atomic Number

Number of protons in nucleus. Defines the element. All atoms of same element have same atomic number.

Mass Number

Total protons + neutrons. Different isotopes of same element have different mass numbers.

Electron Shells

First shell: max 2 electrons. Second shell: max 8 electrons. Third shell: max 8 electrons (in simple model).

Ionic Bonding

Electrons transferred from metal to nonmetal. Creates positive and negative ions. Strong electrostatic attraction.

Covalent Bonding

Electrons shared between atoms. Both atoms gain stability. Forms molecules. Strong directional bonds.
